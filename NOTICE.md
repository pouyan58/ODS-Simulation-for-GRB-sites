# Notices and attribution

## Engineering status

This software and its supplied results are engineering sensitivity outputs.
They are not a frequency-coordination decision, authorization, site
certification, equipment specification, or operator commitment. Independent
technical, regulatory, security, and legal review is required before using
the model for operational decisions.

## Public technical sources

- NOAA GOES Rebroadcast information:
  <https://www.ospo.noaa.gov/operations/goes/grb/>
- NOAA GOES-R GRB downlink specification:
  <https://goes-r.noaa.gov/users/docs/GRB_downlink.pdf>
- ITU-R S.580-6 provides the earth-station antenna reference pattern.
- APEREC015 Appendix 8 supplies the small-aperture extension implemented by
  the simulator.
- ITU-R S.1528-0 informs the LEO spot-beam reference pattern.
- ITU-R P.618 and P.2108 inform atmospheric and clutter calculations.
- The supplied NRAO-informed calibration CSV is a provisional engineering
  transfer from D2D test evidence at 1990-1995 MHz.

The public baseline contains user-selected and provisional assumptions that
are not disclosed operator parameters, including constellation population,
traffic scheduling, geographic avoidance, beam retasking, EIRP density,
PFD cap, the -27 dBi far-angle receive level, and the -6 dB I/N criterion.
